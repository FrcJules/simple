Simple is a radically simplified phone for older people: a home screen and a messaging app designed for someone who reads little and prefers pictures.

For the older person:
• Four large buttons: Call, Messages, Photo, My photos.
• Loved ones are shown by their photo: recognise the person, touch, confirm, call.
• Messages (SMS, MMS and WhatsApp) in large print, with the sender's photo, read aloud on request.
• Reply by speaking: dictate, check, send. Send a photo in two steps.
• A clear confirmation before every call or message, with a button to cancel.
• Very large text, high contrast, time and battery always visible. Works with Android's enlarged font.

For the family:
• Nothing changes for you: keep calling, texting and using WhatsApp as usual.
• You choose which loved ones are shown, and their photos, in the family settings, protected by a code only the family knows.
• Security codes and bank alerts received by SMS are hidden and never read aloud.

Privacy:
• No ads, no trackers, no account, no Internet access.
• Your contacts, calls and messages never leave the phone.
• Free and open source: the source code is public.

With your consent, Simple can become the phone's home screen and messaging app; you can go back to your previous apps at any time in Android settings.
